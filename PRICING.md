# 💰 FED TTS Pricing

## FED TTS is FREE and Open Source

FED TTS is released under the **MIT License**, which means:

✅ **Free to use** – For personal and commercial use
✅ **Free to modify** – Customize it for your needs
✅ **Free to distribute** – Share it with others
✅ **Free to sell** – You can even sell modified versions

## Why is it free?

FED TTS uses **zero cloud services** and **zero AI APIs**, which means:

- **No API fees** – No per-minute transcription costs
- **No usage limits** – Transcribe as much as you want
- **No monthly subscriptions** – Pay nothing, ever
- **No hidden costs** – Everything runs on your local machine

The only cost is the electricity to run your computer.
